title: "WBD Board Unanimously Rejects Paramount's Offer"
fact: "The WBD board unanimously determined that Paramount's $30.00 per share tender offer was not in the best interests of WBD and its stockholders and was not a superior proposal to the Netflix merger agreement. It recommended that stockholders reject the offer and filed a Schedule 14D-9 detailing its analysis."
context: "The 14D-9 read like a line-by-line rebuttal: value, leverage, conditionality, and regulatory risk. It met the procedural requirement and built the record the board would need if its 'no' were ever challenged."
