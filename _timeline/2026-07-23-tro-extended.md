title: "Court Extends Restraining Order to August 17, Sets Briefing Schedule"
fact: "On or about July 23, 2026, Judge Araceli Martinez-Olguin extended the temporary restraining order barring Paramount and Warner Bros. from closing the transaction, moving the expiration from August 3 to August 17, 2026. The court found good cause to extend on multiple grounds, including the need to resolve two preliminary injunction motions, the parties' disputes over scheduling, and the proposed format of the preliminary injunction hearing. Paramount's legal team, led by Jeffrey Kessler, proposed a three-day evidentiary hearing during the week of August 17 or August 24. Note: this extension and the briefing schedule described below were superseded one day later. On July 24, 2026, Paramount and the plaintiffs agreed to cancel the August 3 preliminary injunction hearing entirely and filed a standstill agreement extending well beyond this order. See the July 24, 2026 entry."
context: "This entry is preserved for the record, but the practical significance of a two-week TRO extension was overtaken almost immediately by a much larger agreement the following day. It is a useful reminder that in active litigation, a procedural order can be the right characterization of a situation for less than 24 hours."
