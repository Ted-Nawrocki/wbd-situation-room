title: "Netflix Amends Offer to All-Cash Transaction"
fact: "On January 20, 2026, Netflix and WBD amended their merger agreement to all-cash consideration of $27.75 per share, eliminating the stock component, with WBD stockholders also receiving Discovery Global shares after the separation. Equity value remained $72 billion and enterprise value about $82.7 billion. WBD filed its preliminary proxy the same day, targeting a vote by April 2026. Five days earlier, Vice Chancellor Morgan T. Zurn had denied Paramount's motion to expedite its Delaware suit."
context: "Netflix did not raise its price; it removed Paramount's best argument. All cash eliminated stock-value uncertainty, sped up the vote, and reduced the contest to one question: was Paramount's $2.25 premium worth the whole-company risk?"
