title: "Paramount Launches Hostile Tender Offer"
fact: "Paramount Skydance launched an unsolicited all-cash tender offer for all outstanding WBD shares at $30.00 per share, conditioned on, among other things, WBD entering into Paramount's proposed merger agreement, abandoning the separation plan, and terminating the Netflix merger agreement. The offer was scheduled to expire January 8, 2026, unless extended."
context: "The $30 headline beat Netflix's $27.75, but the conditions meant the offer could not close without the board's cooperation. It was built to pressure the board, not bypass it."
