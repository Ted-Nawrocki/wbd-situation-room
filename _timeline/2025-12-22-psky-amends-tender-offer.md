title: "Paramount Amends Tender Offer, Adds Larry Ellison Personal Guarantee"
fact: "Paramount amended its $30.00 per share tender offer to add a $40.4 billion personal equity financing guarantee from Larry Ellison, raise its reverse termination fee from $5.0 billion to $5.8 billion to match Netflix, and provide more interim operating flexibility. The price was unchanged. WBD said its board would review the amended offer."
context: "Paramount answered the two objections the board had put in writing: financing certainty and a smaller reverse termination fee. That forced the board to evaluate the offer again on its merits."
