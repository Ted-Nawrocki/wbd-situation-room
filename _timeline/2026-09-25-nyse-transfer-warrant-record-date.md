title: "Paramount Moves Its Listing to the NYSE and Sets the Warrant Record Date"
fact: "On September 25, 2026, Paramount filed an 8-K announcing a voluntary transfer of its Class B listing from Nasdaq to the NYSE, with Nasdaq trading ending on or about October 5 and NYSE trading beginning on or about October 6. The filing gives no reason. It also set October 5 as the record date for about 470 million warrants, one per eligible Class B share, with an expected issue date of October 13 and ex-date of October 14, contingent on the WBD closing. The Ellisons, Gerald Cardinale, the Ellison Trust, RedBird, and affiliates are excluded. The exercise price is the 20-day VWAP ending three business days before closing, between $12.00 and $16.02. The warrants have a 10-year term, will be registered on Form S-3, and will list on the NYSE."
context: "The record date falls on Nasdaq's last day; the entitlement trading, issue date, and ex-date fall on the NYSE. With the ex-date after the issue date, due bills follow every trade from October 6 to 13."
