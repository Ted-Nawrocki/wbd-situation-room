title: "Paramount Launches Cash Tender Offers and Exchange Offers on WBD Notes"
fact: "On May 19, 2026, Paramount launched cash tender offers for two short-dated series (the 3.755% notes due 2027 and 3.950% notes due 2028, up to $2.4 billion) and exchange offers for thirteen longer-dated USD and euro series due 2029 to 2052 (up to $12.8 billion) into new Paramount second-lien secured notes with matching coupons and maturities. Both are any-and-all with no proration. The exchange offers are limited to QIBs under Rule 144A and non-U.S. persons under Regulation S. Participation requires prior consent delivery, after which holders receive a temporary CUSIP or ISIN. Settlement is conditioned on the acquisition closing."
context: "Short-dated notes get cash; long-dated notes get Paramount second-lien paper. Exchanging holders keep the coupon but take on a different, more leveraged credit, and settlement waits for the closing, not the expiration date."
