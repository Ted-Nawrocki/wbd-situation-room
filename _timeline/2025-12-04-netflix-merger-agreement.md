title: "Netflix Signs Definitive Merger Agreement"
fact: "WBD and Netflix entered into a definitive merger agreement for Netflix to acquire the WBD Streaming & Studios business following the separation of the Global Networks business. WBD stockholders would receive $23.25 in cash plus Netflix voting stock per share, in addition to shares of the separated Discovery Global. The WBD board unanimously approved the transaction."
context: "Netflix signed while Paramount was still being turned away. The structure built on WBD's June separation plan: spin off Discovery Global, then merge studios and streaming into Netflix."
