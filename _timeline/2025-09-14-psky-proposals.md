title: "Paramount Makes Repeated Acquisition Proposals"
fact: "Between September 14 and November 20, 2025, Paramount Skydance made four progressively higher proposals to acquire WBD, rising from about $19.00 to $25.50 per share. Each mixed cash with non-voting Paramount Class B stock. The WBD board rejected each, citing valuation, the uncertain value of the stock consideration, governance concerns, and a lack of committed equity financing."
context: "Four proposals in two months showed persistent interest and persistent objections. Each rejection got more specific, and by November negotiation had run its course, setting up the hostile tender."
