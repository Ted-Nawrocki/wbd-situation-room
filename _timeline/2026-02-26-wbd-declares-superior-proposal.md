title: "WBD Board Determines Paramount's $31.00 Offer Is a Superior Proposal"
fact: "On February 26, 2026, after receiving a contractual waiver from Netflix, WBD's board reopened talks with Paramount and unanimously determined that Paramount's revised all-cash offer of $31.00 per share, valuing the entire enterprise at about $110.9 billion, was a Superior Proposal under the Netflix agreement. The price was $3.25 above Netflix's $27.75 and required no separation. The determination triggered Netflix's matching rights."
context: "The Netflix waiver was a choice, not a formality. At $31.00 for the whole company, matching meant paying for cable networks Netflix had no use for, and a $2.8 billion termination fee made walking away rational."
