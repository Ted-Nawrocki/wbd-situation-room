title: "Netflix Declines to Match; WBD Signs with Paramount at $31.00 per Share"
fact: "On February 27, 2026, Netflix co-CEOs Ted Sarandos and Greg Peters announced Netflix was 'declining to match.' WBD and Paramount Skydance signed a definitive Agreement and Plan of Merger at $31.00 per share in cash for the entire company, an enterprise value of about $110.9 billion, with no separation required. The agreement's outside date is March 4, 2027, extendable to June 4, 2027. Paramount agreed to a $7 billion reverse termination fee if the deal fails for regulatory reasons and funded the $2.8 billion termination fee owed to Netflix. Correction: an earlier version of this entry listed the reverse termination fee as $5.8 billion, the figure from the Netflix agreement."
context: "'Declining to match' framed Netflix's exit as discipline, and the economics backed it. WBD holders moved from $27.75 plus Discovery Global shares to $31.00 in cash for everything."
