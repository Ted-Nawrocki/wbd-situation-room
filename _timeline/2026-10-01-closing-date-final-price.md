title: "Closing Set for October 6 at $31.01666668 per WBD Share"
fact: "On October 1, 2026, Paramount and WBD announced that the merger is expected to close on October 6, 2026, subject to customary closing conditions. At the effective time, each WBD share converts into the right to receive $31.00 in cash plus Ticking Consideration of $0.00277778 for each calendar day after September 30, 2026 through the closing date. If the deal closes October 6, WBD shareholders receive $31.01666668 per share. The announcement was filed as Exhibit 99.2 to a Paramount Form 8-K."
context: "Six days of ticking fees add under two cents a share, but every payment system has to carry that number to eight decimals. October 6 also lands on the first day of NYSE trading for Paramount, the day after the warrant record date, and starts the settlement clock for the WBD note offers."
