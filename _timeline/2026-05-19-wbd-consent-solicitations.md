title: "WBD Subsidiaries Launch Consent Solicitations Across 17 Series of Notes"
fact: "On May 19, 2026, Discovery Global Holdings, Inc. and Discovery Communications, LLC launched consent solicitations across 17 series of notes, rates 3.950% to 5.300%, maturities 2027 to 2052. The main amendment extends the deadline to commence a Junior Lien Exchange offer from December 30, 2026 to March 4, 2027. Consents were due by 5:00 p.m. New York time on May 26, 2026, with a payment of $2.50 per $1,000 (EUR 2.50 per EUR 1,000), funded by Paramount, on or about May 29. Delivering consent was required to participate in Paramount's concurrent tender and exchange offers, which cover 15 of the 17 series; SEC-registered tranches of the 2027 and 2028 notes could consent but were not eligible for those offers."
context: "The amendment only moved a deadline, but it gated everything else. Missing May 26 meant losing access to both Paramount offers for that series."
