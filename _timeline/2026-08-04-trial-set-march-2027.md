fact: "On August 4, 2026, U.S. District Judge Araceli Martínez-Olguín set the trial in the state coalition's antitrust lawsuit for March 2 to March 19, 2027, in Oakland. Paramount had sought a November 2026 start; the state attorneys general and the Writers Guild of America had proposed April 2027. The trial date fell within the standstill window Paramount agreed to on July 24, 2026 and ahead of the merger agreement's outside date of June 4, 2027. Note: this trial would be vacated if the court approves the proposed consent decree filed September 21, 2026. See the September 21 and September 24 entries."
context: "A November trial would have tested the merits before the ticking fee accrued for long. March pushed that math against Paramount, guaranteeing several quarters of accrual regardless of outcome. In hindsight, that pressure is part of why the case settled before trial."
