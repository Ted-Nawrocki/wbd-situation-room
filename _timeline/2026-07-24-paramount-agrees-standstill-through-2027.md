title: "Paramount Agrees Not to Close Until June 2027 or Five Days After the Lawsuits Resolve"
fact: "On July 24, 2026, Paramount filed an agreement not to close until June 1, 2027, or five days after the state and WGA lawsuits resolve, whichever came first. The August 3 hearing was cancelled and the parties agreed to file a trial scheduling statement by July 31. The ticking fee of $0.25 per share per quarter would still begin accruing after September 30, 2026."
context: "Paramount chose a trial over an emergency hearing. With a $7 billion reverse termination fee against roughly $650 million a quarter in ticking fees, litigating made more sense than walking away."
