title: "Paramount Prices the Acquisition Debt"
fact: "On September 28, 2026, Paramount launched about $44.4 billion of senior secured first lien and second lien notes to qualified institutional buyers under Rule 144A and to non-U.S. persons under Regulation S. On September 30, it announced $41.4 billion and EUR 885 million of senior secured notes offerings and priced an incremental term loan B of $8.5 billion, increased from $7.5 billion with a matching reduction in first lien notes, plus EUR 850 million. Second lien tranches include $1.4 billion of 9.125% notes due 2036. Only the first lien notes carry registration rights. Proceeds, with cash on hand, term loans, and the equity financing, will fund the WBD purchase price and repay certain existing debt."
context: "Roughly $50 billion of secured debt priced in one week shows the market will fund the deal, and the 9.125% coupon shows what it charges a buyer carrying this much leverage."
