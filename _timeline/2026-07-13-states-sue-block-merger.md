title: "Twelve States Sue to Block the Merger"
fact: "On July 13, 2026, twelve states led by California Attorney General Rob Bonta sued in the Northern District of California (Case No. 4:26-cv-07116-AMO) to block the merger, alleging Clayton Act violations in wide-release theatrical distribution, blockbuster theatrical distribution, and basic cable licensing. The coalition: Arizona, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Nevada, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, Oregon, and Washington. The Writers Guild of America filed a separate suit the next day."
context: "State attorneys general enforce antitrust law independently of the DOJ. Five weeks after federal clearance, the deal was back in front of a judge with the power to stop the closing."
