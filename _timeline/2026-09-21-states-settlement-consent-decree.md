title: "Paramount and Twelve States Settle; Five-Year Consent Decree Proposed"
fact: "On September 21, 2026, Paramount, WBD, and the twelve states filed a proposed five-year consent decree and asked the court to dissolve the order barring the closing. Terms include at least $300 million a year in additional U.S. film production; at least 30 theatrical releases a year in years one and two and 32 thereafter, with a 45-day window for wide releases; separate negotiation of Paramount and Warner Bros. cable channels, with divestiture of certain channels for material violations; no sale of the Los Angeles County studio lots; a News Editorial Independence Board for CBS News and CNN; independent monitoring; and $40 million for the states' costs. The WGA consented."
context: "The states traded a trial they might have won for five years of monitored behavioral commitments, with divestiture held back as a penalty. Ticking fees starting October 1 gave both sides a reason to settle."
