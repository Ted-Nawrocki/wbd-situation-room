title: "Paramount Files Lawsuit in Delaware Chancery Court"
fact: "On January 13, 2026, Paramount filed suit in the Delaware Court of Chancery against WBD, CEO David Zaslav, and the board, alleging breach of fiduciary duty and seeking disclosure of the financial analysis behind the board's recommendation of the Netflix transaction. Paramount moved for expedited proceedings, citing its January 21 tender deadline, and did not seek to halt the Netflix deal. It also announced plans to nominate directors for WBD's 2026 annual meeting. WBD called the suit meritless."
context: "Disclosure suits during live deals are rarely about winning on day one. By asking only for information, Paramount kept the request credible while building pressure and a record for shareholders."
