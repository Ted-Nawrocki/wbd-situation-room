In three filings over three days, every remaining piece fell into place: the court, the debt, and the date.

## Just the facts

- September 30: the court entered the consent decree and dissolved the order barring the closing
- September 30: Paramount announced $41.4 billion and EUR 885 million of senior secured notes offerings and priced an $8.5 billion and EUR 850 million term loan B
- October 1: closing expected October 6, 2026, subject to customary conditions
- Each WBD share receives $31.00 plus $0.00277778 for each day after September 30; at an October 6 closing, $31.01666668 per share
- October 6 is also the expected first day of NYSE trading for Paramount Class B shares, the day after the warrant record date

## Ted's Takes

After ten months, the ending is a date and a number. For the people processing it, October 6 is a beginning: the merger payout carried to eight decimals, a new primary listing venue, a warrant entitlement period with due bills, and settlement of the WBD note offers that have waited since May, all in the same week.
