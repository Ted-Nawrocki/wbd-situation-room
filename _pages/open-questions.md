**As of October 1, 2026**

## Active

**1. Does the deal close on October 6 as scheduled?**
Only customary conditions remain. A slip would move the per-share consideration, which rises by $0.00277778 for each day after September 30, and could move the warrant record and issue dates.

**2. How do the WBD note offers settle?**
How much of the roughly $12.7 billion of exchange-eligible notes and $2.42 billion of tender-eligible notes comes in, and what remains outstanding as legacy WBD subsidiary debt after closing?

**3. Where does the warrant exercise price land?**
It is set by the 20-day VWAP ending three business days before closing, between $12.00 and $16.02.

**4. How smoothly does the listing move run alongside the warrant entitlement period?**
The record date falls on Nasdaq's last day, while due bills, the issue date, and the ex-date fall on the NYSE.

**5. Can the combined company delever on schedule?**
The capital structure now carries roughly $50 billion of newly priced secured debt, including second lien notes with coupons as high as 9.125%.

**6. Does the consent decree work as designed?**
Five years of behavioral commitments with monitoring and divestiture as the penalty. Critics, including Senator Booker and Connecticut's attorney general, argued it does not go far enough.

**7. What happens to CNN and CBS News?**
The decree requires a News Editorial Independence Board within 180 days of closing.
