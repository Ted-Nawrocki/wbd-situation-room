*Updated October 1, 2026. The regulatory and litigation items tracked on this page since July are resolved. The items below cover closing week and what follows.*

## Closing week

**October 5, 2026:** Last Nasdaq trading day for Paramount Class B shares and record date for about 470 million warrants.

**October 6, 2026:** Expected closing. Each WBD share converts into $31.01666668 in cash at an October 6 closing. Expected first NYSE trading day for Paramount Class B shares.

**October 13 and 14, 2026:** Expected warrant issue date and ex-date. Shares traded from October 6 to 13 carry the warrant entitlement.

**On or shortly after closing:** Settlement of Paramount's cash tender offers and exchange offers for WBD notes.

## After closing

**WBD delisting.** WBD common stock stops trading once the merger is effective.

**Consent decree compliance.** Five years of commitments on theatrical output, U.S. production spending, separate cable negotiations, and studio lots, with an independent monitoring trustee and divestiture as the penalty for material violations.

**News Editorial Independence Board.** Required for CBS News and CNN within 180 days of closing.

**HBO Max and Paramount+.** Paramount has said it will combine the two services into one platform.

**Leverage.** Roughly $50 billion of new secured debt was priced in the final week. Watch progress against Paramount's deleveraging commitments to the ratings agencies.

**Warrants.** The exercise price is set by the 20-day VWAP ending three business days before closing, between $12.00 and $16.02, and the warrants run 10 years.
