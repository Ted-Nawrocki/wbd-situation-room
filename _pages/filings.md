## State Antitrust Litigation and Settlement

### State of California, et al. v. Paramount Skydance Corporation and Warner Bros. Discovery, Inc.
Case No. 4:26-cv-07116-AMO, U.S. District Court, Northern District of California

**Complaint** (Filed July 13, 2026)  
Twelve-state coalition led by California alleging Clayton Act Section 7 violations in theatrical distribution and basic cable licensing  
[California Attorney General](https://oag.ca.gov/)

**Order Granting Temporary Restraining Order** (July 20, 2026)  
[Order (PDF) via California Attorney General](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/attachments/press-docs/ordergranting27motionfortemporaryrestraining.pdf)

**Order Extending Temporary Restraining Order** (July 23, 2026)  
Extended to August 17, 2026; superseded by the July 24 standstill

**Standstill Agreement** (July 24, 2026)  
Paramount agreed not to close until the earlier of five days after the merits determination or June 1, 2027; preliminary injunction schedule cancelled  
[PACER](https://www.pacer.gov)

**Trial Scheduling Order** (August 4, 2026)  
Trial set for March 2 to March 19, 2027; would be vacated if the consent decree is entered

**Proposed Consent Decree and Joint Motion to Dissolve Order** (Filed September 21, 2026)  
Five-year behavioral decree covering film output, U.S. production spending, cable negotiations, studio lots, and news editorial independence  
[Text of proposed consent decree via Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/09/paramount-warner-bros-discovery-consent-decree-1237109397/)

**Order Setting Hearing on Proposed Consent Decree; Order Vacating Bond Motion Hearing** (September 22, 2026)  
Hearing set for September 24; hearing on Paramount's $1.88 billion bond motion vacated

**Order Granting Motions to File Amicus Briefs** (September 24, 2026)  
Amicus briefs due 12:01 a.m. Pacific, September 25, 2026

**Hearing on Proposed Consent Decree** (September 24, 2026)  
No ruling issued; parties ordered to respond to Senator Cory Booker's letter by noon Pacific, September 28, 2026

**Writers Guild of America, Separate Action** (Filed July 14, 2026)  
WGA consented to the deal following the state settlement  
[PACER](https://www.pacer.gov)
