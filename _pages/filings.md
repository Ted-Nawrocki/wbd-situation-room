## Regulatory and Litigation (June - July 2026)

### DOJ Antitrust Division

**Statement of the Department of Justice Antitrust Division on the Closing of Its Investigation** (June 12, 2026)  
Official statement closing the eight-month investigation without divestitures, behavioral remedies, or conditions  
[U.S. Department of Justice, Office of Public Affairs](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/statement-department-justice-antitrust-division-closing-its-investigation-merger-paramount)

### State Attorneys General Litigation

**Complaint -- State of California, et al. v. Paramount Skydance Corporation and Warner Bros. Discovery, Inc.** (Filed July 13, 2026)  
Case No. 4:26-cv-07116-AMO, U.S. District Court, Northern District of California. Twelve-state coalition led by California Attorney General Rob Bonta, alleging Clayton Act violations in wide-release theatrical distribution, blockbuster theatrical distribution, and basic cable channel licensing  
[California Attorney General -- Press Materials](https://oag.ca.gov/)

**Order Granting Motion for Temporary Restraining Order** (Filed July 20, 2026)  
Case No. 4:26-cv-07116-AMO, N.D. Cal. Judge Araceli Martinez-Olguin's order barring Paramount and WBD from closing the transaction pending further proceedings  
[Order (PDF) via California Attorney General](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/attachments/press-docs/ordergranting27motionfortemporaryrestraining.pdf)

**Order Extending Temporary Restraining Order** (On or about July 23, 2026)  
Case No. 4:26-cv-07116-AMO, N.D. Cal. Extension of the TRO from August 3 to August 17, 2026. Superseded the following day by the standstill agreement below.

**Stipulation and Standstill Agreement** (Filed July 24, 2026)  
Case No. 4:26-cv-07116-AMO, N.D. Cal. Paramount's agreement not to close the transaction until June 1, 2027, or five days after the state and Writers Guild of America lawsuits resolve, whichever comes first. The parties agreed to cancel the scheduled preliminary injunction hearing and to file a joint statement on trial scheduling by July 31, 2026  
[U.S. District Court, Northern District of California -- PACER](https://www.pacer.gov)

### European Commission

**European Commission Approves Paramount Skydance Corporation Acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery** (July 22, 2026)  
Formal clearance under the EU Merger Regulation, conditioned on Paramount's exit from its United International Pictures joint venture with Universal Pictures in Europe within 13 months of closing  
[Paramount Skydance press release via PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/european-commission-approves-paramount-skydance-corporation-acquisition-of-warner-bros-discovery-marking-major-milestone-towards-completion-302832497.html)
